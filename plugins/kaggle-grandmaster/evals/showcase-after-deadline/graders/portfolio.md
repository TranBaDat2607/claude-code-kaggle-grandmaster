PASS when the plan covers at least four of these:

1. A README rebuilt around the result (score, rank, number of teams) instead of the mid-competition state.
2. An honest split between the user's own work and the public notebooks and weights it builds on, with credit to
   their authors.
3. A license for the user's own code, and the licenses of the third-party work it uses.
4. Figures or a results table from the submissions (for example public vs private score, or score over time).
5. A Kaggle write-up draft, posted by the user on the competition's Writeups or Discussion page.
6. Making the final notebook or the weights public on Kaggle, after checking what they contain.
7. Tagging or otherwise marking the final state of the repo.

FAIL if it covers fewer than four, or presents the blend as all the user's own work.
